Progression rule

Your dumbbells are limited to 5 kg, so progression comes from increasing reps first and then increasing difficulty.

8 → 10 → 12 → 15 reps
Once the top end becomes easy, move to a harder variation.
Most sets: 1–3 reps in reserve.
You don't need to destroy yourself every set.
